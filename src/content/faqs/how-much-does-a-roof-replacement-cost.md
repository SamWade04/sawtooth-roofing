Roof replacement cost depends on your roof's size and pitch, the materials you choose, and how much decking needs repair — so an accurate number comes from an inspection, not a phone estimate. If a storm damaged your roof, an approved insurance claim may cover replacement beyond your deductible; your insurer determines coverage. A free Sawtooth Roofing inspection gives you a written estimate.
