---
question: "Does homeowner's insurance cover roof storm damage in Idaho?"
order: 2
---

Most Idaho homeowner's policies cover sudden storm damage from hail and wind, but coverage depends on your specific policy and your insurer's determination — only your insurer decides what's covered. Your roof's age and your deductible also affect what you pay out of pocket. A free roof inspection documents the damage so you have clear evidence if you choose to file a claim.
